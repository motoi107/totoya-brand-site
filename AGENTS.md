# TOTOYA website review workflow

- Preserve TOTOYA's established, restrained, video-led restaurant style.
- Official subtitle: Japanese Negitoro & Seafood Bowls.
- Do not display the Japanese fish-shop label or introduce a fish-shop etymology.
- Show complete photographs and video frames without cropping food to fill a fixed frame. The owner will refine photo selection.
- Prepare changes on a review branch. Verify phone and desktop layouts separately and provide a reviewable preview of each.
- Do not update the production main branch or www.totoyahawaii.com until the owner explicitly approves the reviewed revision. Approval for an earlier revision does not approve a later redesign.
- A separate private review preview may be prepared; label it as a draft and keep the production site unchanged.
