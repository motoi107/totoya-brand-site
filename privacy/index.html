Privacy at Totoya.

Finding a restaurant

If you choose “Use my location,” your browser asks for permission. This page uses your coordinates on your device to find the closest Totoya by straight-line distance. Totoya does not save your coordinates. You can also choose a starting area, browse every location or open directions without sharing your location with Totoya.

Maps & directions

When the restaurant map loads map tiles, your browser connects to OpenStreetMap. Its servers receive the connection information needed to serve those tiles, including your IP address. Read the OpenStreetMap Foundation privacy policy for details.

Directions open in Google Maps. If you choose a named starting area, the directions link includes that area and your selected restaurant.

Reservations & orders

Our reservation and pickup links take you to third-party services. When you open one of those websites or enter information there, that provider’s privacy practices apply. Review the provider’s privacy notice before you submit a booking or order.

Contact & applications

Company enquiries and job applications go to Funergy Group. You can contact the team by email or through the company website. For questions about this website or how your information is handled, visit our contact page.
