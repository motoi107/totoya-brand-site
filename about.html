Our story

Japanese at heart.
At home in Hawai‘i.

TOTOYA brings Japanese seafood bowls to the neighborhoods of O‘ahu. A place to pause, share a meal, and enjoy what’s in your bowl.

魚屋 · TOTOYA

Seafood is our starting point.

Our name means “fish shop.” Our menu brings seafood together with rice in kaisendon: a Japanese bowl made for enjoying one bite at a time.

A bowl for your moment.

From original negitoro to salmon roe, uni, and snow crab, our menu offers plenty to explore. Meet over lunch, settle in for dinner, or pick up a favorite to enjoy at home.

Part of your neighborhood.

Visit us in Kaimuki, Ala Moana / Piikoi, and Aiea. We are also preparing to welcome the Kapolei community, with an opening planned for late February.
