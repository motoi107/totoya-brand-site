About TOTOYA

Japanese Negitoro & Seafood Bowls

TOTOYA serves negitoro and seafood bowls in Kaimuki, Ala Moana / Piikoi, and Aiea.

Our menu

Our original negitoro bowls include combinations of salmon roe, uni, and snow crab. Explore the sashimi selection, or choose the Kaimuki-exclusive Hand-roll Set to enjoy at home.

Visit us

Join us for lunch or dinner, or order takeout. Kaimuki and Aiea now accept reservations. Our Kapolei location is planned to open in late February.
